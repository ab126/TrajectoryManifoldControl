# TrajectoryManifoldControl: validation summary

October 14, 2026. **The original demonstration reproduces; the fitted linear operating mode usually outperforms the hybrid on the tested balancing task.** The original campaign is complete. The literature extension has substantial completed evidence but is **stopped and incomplete**: its supervisor detected a credit-balance decrease and exited. See [current status](../results_literature/STATUS); no restart is authorized.

**Method and notation.** Following the team's [implementation note](../../../trajectory_manifold_implementation.tex), the sampled system is $x_{k+1}=f(x_k,u_k)$, with four states, one input and horizon $N$. Coordinates $q=\operatorname{col}(x_0,\mathbf u)$ combine the initial state and planned input sequence. The learned decoder $\widehat\Phi_{N,\vartheta}(q)$ predicts a trajectory using the learned transition $\widehat f_\vartheta$. Network parameters are $\vartheta$; $\theta$ denotes pendulum angle. **H** is the full hybrid controller: predictive planning plus the linear operating mode. **L** applies that same mode, $\kappa_{\rm loc}(x)=\operatorname{clip}(-Kx,-10,10)$, everywhere. Its model $x_{k+1}\approx\widehat A x_k+\widehat B u_k$ is fitted to 6,000 nearby training transitions; a linear-quadratic regulator computes $K$. Neither fit uses exact plant equations or derivatives.

**Task and settings.** Nominal tests observe noiseless cart position/velocity and rod angle/angular velocity every 20 ms, with force capped at ±98.1 N. Original H uses $N=30$ (0.6 seconds), three 192-unit hidden layers, 3,500 collected episodes and 100 training epochs. Its cross-entropy sampling optimizer uses 256 candidates, 32 elites and three iterations, normally applying five inputs before replanning. The linear mode takes priority when $x^\top Qx<0.75$, with $Q=\operatorname{diag}(0.02,30,100,10)$, $R=0.03$ and $Q_f=12Q$. States use the note's normalized coordinates; endpoint speeds and plots use physical units. This is a favorable full-state, strong-actuation simulation setting.

**Success and recovery.** At 14 seconds, angle and physical angular speed must stay within ±0.02 radians and ±0.05 radians/second throughout the final three seconds, with no earlier crossing of ±90°. Cart recentering is not required. This primary criterion is preserved. A separate terminal-recovery criterion permits earlier crossings: H then scores **78, 73, 127/200**, while L remains **122, 130, 128/200** on the original random states. See [endpoint sensitivity](../results_literature/horizontal_sensitivity/README.md). Longer runs remain separate.

SMC's small gains over L are descriptive; no statistically resolved advantage is established. Across ten additional seeds, H succeeds on four or five of the six authored from-rest angles (±15°, ±30°, ±60°), and L on all six. On the 1° from-rest grid, L succeeds throughout −68°…+68° in all three seeds; both fail beyond ±70° through ±85° under the primary criterion. H has gaps; its contiguous all-seed success interval is −36°…+30°. These are finite-grid observations, not certified boundaries.

**Other comparisons have different coverage.** On the first 50 original random states, controlled Deep Koopman (learned state features with latent dynamics) scores **0/50 per seed** natively and **4, 12, 26/50** with L. Its matched neural first-action planner with L scores **11, 6, 22/50**. **DFINE**, a learned hidden-state model with causal filtering, scores **0/50 per seed** for each of three full-state controllers. **PETS**, probabilistic ensembles with trajectory sampling, and its deterministic ensemble reference each score **0/50 per seed**. Their 200-state original-set comparisons are incomplete. The separate position/angle-only track also has zero successes in its completed 50-state cells; its observations differ from full-state H/L. [Exact coverage and counts](../results_literature/CONTROLLER_COUNTS.md) retain individual arms, fresh holdouts and partial denominators.

Data-enabled Predictive Control (**DeePC**) was evaluated on a separate 3,281 × 32 training collection. Later kernelized and linear-kernel controllers each score **0/200 per seed** on the original test states; matched references remain incomplete. In the original linear-span DeePC study, all 866,538 optimization calls passed acceptance checks, but [six identical-input replays](E4/deepc_matched_plan_replays.json) showed predicted upright endpoints becoming falls. Feasible optimization need not imply physically valid prediction. These task adaptations do not establish failure of entire algorithm families; forecast accuracy alone does not establish successful control.

**Limits and unfinished work.** No numerical stability region is certified. Some fitted gains destabilize the true upright dynamics despite stabilizing their fitted models; the [review](DOCUMENTATION_REVIEW.md) and [guarantee handoff](STABILITY_GUARANTEE_HANDOFF.md) explain the evidence. Nominal simulation omits actual computation delay; three fitting seeds and small long-run subsets limit generalization. Solver rejections, equilibrium bias, [source adaptations](../results_literature/sources/PORT_NOTES.md) and incomplete reference coverage qualify baseline conclusions. PETS fresh-state expansion, SINDy stress completion, some matched references and the collaborator's 40-step/six-iteration 80° reproduction remain incomplete or unconfirmed. PID, proportional–integral–derivative control, is prepared but untested. The [literature report](../results_literature/RESULTS.md), [original report](RESULTS.md), [status and outstanding-work record](../results_literature/reviews/documentation_status_20261014.json) and [supervisor log](../results_literature/supervisor.log) provide the evidence trail. Raw trajectories and weights require a separate local archive.
