# Saved-result initial-state regime figures

Generated by `validation/experiments/briefing_regime_figures.py`, which reads existing CSVs only. No simulation, training, interpolation, smoothing, or inferred unobserved outcomes. PNG and vector PDF versions are supplied.

**Figure 1 (`initial_state_regimes`):** Empirical primary-success frequency at each initial state, evaluated for 14 s under three whole-run seeds (0, 1, 2). Top: E1 D's 41 × 41 angle/angular-rate grid; bottom: E1 B's 171 from-rest angles, −85° to 85° at 1° spacing. Initial cart position and velocity are zero throughout these slices. Color is exactly 0/3, 1/3, 2/3, or 3/3 successful runs at the same state, not a calibrated probability, independent repeated rollout estimate, or general stability guarantee. Stored angles are converted to degrees; stored angular rates are multiplied by √9.81 to rad/s, consistent with `campaign.py`'s physical-rate metric. H is the learned-predictor hybrid controller; L is the local linear controller; M uses the learned planner only with a five-step action queue; M1 uses the learned planner only and replans every step; H* is H with the true plant predictor.

**Figure 2 (`matched_baseline_regimes`):** Nominal E4 and E6 baseline comparisons on the same initial states but different training collections; comparisons must be made within each source block. E4 uses a 3,281-episode × 32-transition collection; E6 uses the original 3,500-episode × 30-transition collection. The two blocks are visibly separated and every row identifies its source. Results are shown on C50 (50 states) and A (six from-rest states). Each column represents one saved initial state, sorted by angle within its own cohort, and each cell uses exactly seeds 0, 1, 2 at 14 s. Bracketed counts report successes across all runs in that cohort. E4 supplies H, L, NN planner M1, five-member ensemble E5, and six DeePC settings; E6 supplies its own H and L references, nominal SMC, and its zero-correction control. Its H/L references must be used when comparing SMC against those arms; the E4 H/L rows use the other training collection. The four lower panels give the full initial-state coordinates in physical units. Connecting lines in these metadata panels only aid reading the ordered cases; they do not interpolate success or imply a trajectory. C50 varies all four state coordinates, so no universal angular threshold can be inferred. These baselines have no saved dense angle/rate grid; no dense baseline map is fabricated.

Exact from-rest observations on the saved 1° B grid:

- L: at least one success at every angle −70° through +70°; all three succeed at every angle −68° through +68°. No success at sampled |θ₀| ≥ 71° (90 runs).
- H: any success occurs only within −67° to +54°, with gaps; all three succeed continuously from −36° through +30°, plus −38°, +32°, +33°. No success outside [−67°, +54°] (147 runs). The bounds are observed extrema, not a contiguous guaranteed basin.
- H*: any success at −48° and every angle −46° through +49°; all three succeed from −43° through +39° and at +41°, +42°. No success outside [−48°, +49°] (219 runs).
- M and M1: zero successes in all 513 B runs and all 5,043 D runs each.

D successful runs / 5,043: H 1,485; L 3,247; M 0; M1 0; H* 1,547. D cells with any success / 1,681: H 746; L 1,089; M 0; M1 0; H* 582.

`regime_statistics.json` preserves every exact B angle with any success and with all-three success, plus D and matched-baseline counts. Primary success is the campaign's stored angular-tail balance criterion combined with no fall (finite states/inputs and |θ| < π/2 throughout). Cart-rest is descriptive and is not part of primary success.
